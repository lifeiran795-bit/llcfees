The part most guides leave out

Rhode Island's annual report is $50, due May 1 for every entity. The state is small enough that people assume there is no annual obligation — there is, and there is a late fee.

Common questions

How much does it cost to form a Rhode Island LLC?

$150, paid once to the state when the articles are filed. Anything advertised below that is usually a promotional price from a formation service, which recovers the difference elsewhere.

What does a Rhode Island LLC cost every year after that?

$50 — annual report, due may 1 every year.

What happens if the Rhode Island annual filing is late?

The state adds a penalty and, if it stays unpaid, eventually dissolves the LLC. In most states reinstatement costs more than the fee ever did, and the company is not in good standing while it is delinquent.

Do I need a registered agent in Rhode Island?

Yes. Every state requires a registered agent with a physical street address in that state to receive legal service. A mail-forwarding address is not enough in most states, and a few require an agent who is physically present.
