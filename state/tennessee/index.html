The part most guides leave out

Tennessee prices per member: $50 each with a $300 minimum, and the same formula applies every year to the annual report. A four-member LLC pays $300 to form and $300 a year before any tax — which is why it looks cheap in a one-column comparison and is not.

Common questions

How much does it cost to form a Tennessee LLC?

$300, paid once to the state when the articles are filed. Anything advertised below that is usually a promotional price from a formation service, which recovers the difference elsewhere.

What does it cost every year after that?

$300 — annual report ($50 per member, $300 minimum), due april 1 for calendar-year entities.

What happens if the annual filing is late?

The state adds a penalty and, if it stays unpaid, eventually dissolves the LLC. In most states reinstatement costs more than the fee ever did, and the company is not in good standing while it is delinquent.

Do I need a registered agent in Tennessee?

Yes. Every state requires a registered agent with a physical street address in that state to receive legal service. A mail-forwarding address is not enough in most states, and a few require an agent who is physically present.
