How this data is maintained

LLC fees change. States raise them, rename forms, and change deadlines without notice. Each page therefore stores the date it was last checked and links straight to the official filing page, so you can confirm the number yourself in one click.

Pages marked unverified exist so the structure is public while the research is still in progress. Do not rely on them.

This site publishes fee figures and links to official state sources. It is not legal, tax or accounting advice.
