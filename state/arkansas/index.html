The part most guides leave out

Arkansas looks like a $45 state until you read the annual column: the franchise tax is $150, more than three times the formation fee. It is due May 1 every year regardless of when you formed.

Common questions

How much does it cost to form a Arkansas LLC?

$45, paid once to the state when the articles are filed. Anything advertised below that is usually a promotional price from a formation service, which recovers the difference elsewhere.

What does it cost every year after that?

$150 — annual franchise tax, due may 1 every year.

What happens if the annual filing is late?

The state adds a penalty and, if it stays unpaid, eventually dissolves the LLC. In most states reinstatement costs more than the fee ever did, and the company is not in good standing while it is delinquent.

Do I need a registered agent in Arkansas?

Yes. Every state requires a registered agent with a physical street address in that state to receive legal service. A mail-forwarding address is not enough in most states, and a few require an agent who is physically present.
