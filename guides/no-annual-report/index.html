States with no annual report for LLCs

Where the claim breaks down

Idaho and Mississippi both appear in "free" lists, and both still require a filing. In both states a missed $0 report ends in administrative dissolution exactly like a missed paid one. Free is not the same as optional.

South Carolina has no state annual report, but counties issue their own annual business licence, and that is enforced locally. The state bill is zero; the local one usually is not.

Arizona has no annual report, but some counties require publishing a notice in a local newspaper shortly after formation. That is a one-off cost, not annual, and it is easy to miss.

If you are willing to pay a little

Five states charge something small and give you a real filing system to work with. For most non-resident owners this is the better trade:

Pennsylvania at $7 is the standout — an annual filing cheaper than a coffee, due September 30 every year. Wyoming at $60 is the one most non-residents pick, because the annual report is a licence tax based on in-state assets rather than a flat fee.

The number that surprises people

The annual fee is rarely the biggest cost of keeping an LLC alive. The registered agent, the state income tax filing, and the accounting bill usually dwarf it. A state with a free annual report and a $0 registered agent requirement does not exist — pick the state on the whole package, not the filing fee.
