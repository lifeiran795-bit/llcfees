The part most guides leave out

Minnesota charges $155 to form and nothing to renew — one of very few states with a free annual renewal. The deadline is fixed at December 31, so it never lines up with your anniversary and is easy to miss even though it costs nothing.

Common questions

How much does it cost to form a Minnesota LLC?

$155, paid once to the state when the articles are filed. Anything advertised below that is usually a promotional price from a formation service, which recovers the difference elsewhere.

What does it cost every year after that?

$0 — annual renewal (no fee), due december 31 every year.

What happens if the annual filing is late?

The state adds a penalty and, if it stays unpaid, eventually dissolves the LLC. In most states reinstatement costs more than the fee ever did, and the company is not in good standing while it is delinquent.

Do I need a registered agent in Minnesota?

Yes. Every state requires a registered agent with a physical street address in that state to receive legal service. A mail-forwarding address is not enough in most states, and a few require an agent who is physically present.
