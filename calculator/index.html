What this calculator leaves out

It counts state filing fees only. It does not include the registered agent (usually $50–$300 a year), the accountant, or any county and city licence. For most owners those three cost more than the state fees shown here — so treat the total as a floor, not a budget.

It also does not model the cost of getting it wrong: a missed annual filing typically means a penalty, and in several states an outright administrative dissolution that costs more to undo than the fee ever did.
