Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

The short version

This site does not have accounts, does not ask for personal information, and does not run its own analytics or tracking cookies. The only data involved is standard web server logging, plus advertising cookies if ads are shown.

What is collected automatically

The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub records standard request logs — IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp — for security and abuse prevention. That data is handled under GitHub's privacy statement, not by us.

Cookies

This site sets no first-party cookies. The calculator runs entirely in your browser and stores nothing.

Advertising

If advertising is enabled in the future, it will be served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info. This page will be updated before any advertising is switched on.

External links

Every state page links to that state's official filing site. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Your rights

Because no personal data is stored by this site, there is nothing to request, correct or delete. If you believe otherwise, contact us and we will look into it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes — for example switching advertising on — will be announced on the homepage.

This site publishes fee figures and links to official state sources. It is not legal, tax or accounting advice.
