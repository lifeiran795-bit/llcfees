About this site

A single-page answer to a question that should take ten seconds to answer and usually takes twenty minutes: what does an LLC actually cost in each US state, once, and every year after that?

Why it exists

Every comparison page leads with the formation fee, because it is the biggest number and the easiest to copy. The number that decides whether an LLC survives its second year is the annual one — and that number is frequently wrong, out of date, or quietly omitting the tax agency entirely.

Delaware is the clearest example. Most pages still say $300 a year, due March 1. The actual figure is $400, due June 1, and Delaware LLCs do not file an annual report at all. That is the sort of error this site is built to avoid.

Where the numbers come from

Each state page links to that state's own Secretary of State, or to the tax agency responsible for the fee, when the two are different. Figures are compiled from those published fee schedules — not from other comparison sites, and not from memory.

3 of 50 state pages have additionally been re-checked line by line against the official source. Those carry a green badge and a date on the page. The rest are compiled but not yet re-checked, and say so.

What this site does not do

It does not sell company formation, does not act as a registered agent, and does not take a commission for steering you toward a particular state. If that ever changes, it will be disclosed on the page where it applies.

It also does not give legal or tax advice. Where you owe tax depends on where you live and where you work, not where you incorporate.

Found an error?

Fees change, and states are not always careful about announcing it. If a number here disagrees with the official page, the official page is right. Send the state, the field and the source link to 476949867@qq.com and it will be corrected.

How the site is built

Static pages, no database, no tracking. It is served from a CDN, which is why it loads in about a second anywhere in the world. The whole site is a few hundred kilobytes.
