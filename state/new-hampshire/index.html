The part most guides leave out

New Hampshire charges $100 to form and $100 a year — the annual report costs as much as starting. The state has no wage income tax, but the Business Profits Tax can still apply to an LLC's earnings.

Common questions

How much does it cost to form a New Hampshire LLC?

$100, paid once to the state when the articles are filed. Anything advertised below that is usually a promotional price from a formation service, which recovers the difference elsewhere.

What does a New Hampshire LLC cost every year after that?

$100 — annual report, due april 1 every year.

What happens if the New Hampshire annual filing is late?

The state adds a penalty and, if it stays unpaid, eventually dissolves the LLC. In most states reinstatement costs more than the fee ever did, and the company is not in good standing while it is delinquent.

Do I need a registered agent in New Hampshire?

Yes. Every state requires a registered agent with a physical street address in that state to receive legal service. A mail-forwarding address is not enough in most states, and a few require an agent who is physically present.
