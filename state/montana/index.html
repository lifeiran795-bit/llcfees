The part most guides leave out

At $35 to form and $20 a year, Montana is one of the cheapest states in the country — which is exactly why it attracts owners who never set foot there. The registered agent is the part that decides whether the LLC survives; a stale address is the most common way these get dissolved.

Common questions

How much does it cost to form a Montana LLC?

$35, paid once to the state when the articles are filed. Anything advertised below that is usually a promotional price from a formation service, which recovers the difference elsewhere.

What does it cost every year after that?

$20 — annual report, due april 15 every year.

What happens if the annual filing is late?

The state adds a penalty and, if it stays unpaid, eventually dissolves the LLC. In most states reinstatement costs more than the fee ever did, and the company is not in good standing while it is delinquent.

Do I need a registered agent in Montana?

Yes. Every state requires a registered agent with a physical street address in that state to receive legal service. A mail-forwarding address is not enough in most states, and a few require an agent who is physically present.
