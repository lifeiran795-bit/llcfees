The part most guides leave out

Iowa files every two years, in odd-numbered years on April 1 — not on your anniversary. Filing online costs $30; the paper form costs $45 for the same thing.

Common questions

How much does it cost to form a Iowa LLC?

$50, paid once to the state when the articles are filed. Anything advertised below that is usually a promotional price from a formation service, which recovers the difference elsewhere.

What does a Iowa LLC cost every year after that?

$30 — biennial report (every 2 years), due april 1 of odd-numbered years.

What happens if the Iowa annual filing is late?

The state adds a penalty and, if it stays unpaid, eventually dissolves the LLC. In most states reinstatement costs more than the fee ever did, and the company is not in good standing while it is delinquent.

Do I need a registered agent in Iowa?

Yes. Every state requires a registered agent with a physical street address in that state to receive legal service. A mail-forwarding address is not enough in most states, and a few require an agent who is physically present.
